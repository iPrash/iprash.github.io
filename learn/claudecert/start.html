    Claude certification pathway

    Three proctored exams in nine weeks, 90 minutes a day

    I take three Claude certification exams in this order: Developer Foundations, Architect Foundations, Architect Professional. I skip Associate. The plan uses 90 minutes a day, six days a week, and finishes by 15 December 2026. That date leaves room for one retake per exam before 31 December, when the free-exam offer for top-tier partners ends.

    Read this page once. Then work one day at a time from the exam page for the current block.

    Part 0 · The plan

    0.1 The four exams

    All facts in this table come from the official exam guides, version 1.0, effective July 2026 (R1 to R4), checked 14 October 2026.

    Exam Code Items List fee Pass Validity
    Associate Foundations CCAO-F 60 $99 720 of 1,000 12 months
    Developer Foundations CCDV-F 53 $125 720 of 1,000 12 months
    Architect Foundations CCAR-F 60, from 4 of 6 scenarios $125 720 of 1,000 12 months
    Architect Professional CCAR-P 63 $175 720 of 1,000 12 months

    Every exam is 120 minutes, proctored by Pearson, and has no mandatory prerequisite. No notes, books or second monitors are allowed. The score report shows percent correct by domain. Use that report to steer the next block.

    Policy facts from the Developer guide (R1, sections 10 to 14), checked 14 October 2026. The other guides use the same policy text:

    • Retake waiting periods are 14 days after the first fail, 30 after the second, 90 after the third. The limit is four attempts per exam in 12 months.
    • Cancel or reschedule up to 24 hours before the appointment. Later changes, and no-shows, forfeit the fee.
    • The name on the ID must match the registration exactly. Fix a mismatch through certifications-support@anthropic.com before scheduling.
    • Renewal before expiry is free, through an unproctored assessment on Partner Academy.
    • Every candidate accepts a non-disclosure agreement covering all questions, answer options and scenarios.

    Facts from the certification FAQ (R6), checked 14 October 2026:

    • Only people at organizations in the Claude Partner Network can register. A personal email address does not work.
    • Select, Preferred and Global Premier partners get 50% off. Global Premier partners pay nothing until 31 December 2026.
    • A retake costs the full fee again.

    Fact from a secondary source (R11, 17 September 2026), not found on a primary page: the official practice exam was retired. The only official practice is the few sample questions in each guide. That is why this site has its own question bank.

    0.2 Why this order

    Step Reason
    1 · Developer The broadest blueprint. Applications and integration is 33%, and much of that is software engineering I already do. It forces me through the API mechanics (streaming, caching, batches, vision) that the Architect exam excludes but assumes.
    2 · Architect Foundations The same technology, tested at mechanic level: Agent SDK hooks, Claude Code rules and skills, CI flags, extraction pipelines. Claude Code is 3% of Developer and 20% of Architect, so my daily Claude Code use pays off here.
    3 · Architect Professional Discovery, governance, RAG, evaluation and stakeholder trade-offs. This is the closest exam to my day job, so it needs the least new learning and gains most from the two exams before it.
    Associate · skipped It does not count toward partner tier eligibility (R5). Its content is a subset of Professional domains 5 and 6. Take it in the December buffer only if all three pass first time.

    0.3 Calendar

    Day 1 is Wednesday 14 October 2026. Sundays are rest days. Saturdays are long days of up to 2 hours.

    Days Dates Block Exam Earliest retake

    4 to 28 17 Oct to 10 Nov Developer Tue 10 Nov Tue 24 Nov
    29 to 46 11 to 28 Nov Architect Foundations Sat 28 Nov Sat 12 Dec
    47 to 62 29 Nov to 15 Dec Architect Professional Tue 15 Dec Tue 29 Dec

    Thanksgiving (26 November) falls inside the Architect block. That is why the Architect exam is on the Saturday after.

    If I fail an exam, I book the retake for the earliest date and keep the next block running. The retake prep is the domain report plus my drills for the weak domains, 30 minutes a day on top of the current block.

    0.4 The daily routine

    Block Minutes What
    Recall 10 Answer 5 questions from my bank, written on earlier days. Mark the ones I miss.
    Build 40 to 45 The day's artifact. Build first, then read.
    Read 30 to 40 The course module or docs named for the day, tied to what I just built.
    Write 10 Write 3 exam-style questions from today's work into the Drills page format. Log gotchas.

    Each week: five build days, one long Saturday (a longer build or a timed mock), and one rest day on Sunday. If a day runs over, I finish the build and drop the read. The read can move to Sunday. The build cannot.

    0.5 The build spine

    All builds live in one private repo called cert-lab. Each build maps to blueprint domains.

    # Build Block Domains
    1 API basics: messages, tokens, models, streaming, vision, caching, batches Developer Dev D2, D5
    2 Prompt lab: a classifier with a test set, few-shot, structured output, validation Developer Dev D6
    3 Tool-use agent: manual loop, then Agent SDK, with a blocking hook Developer Dev D1, D7, D8
    4 MCP server, connected to Claude Code Developer Dev D8
    5 Claude Code team configuration and a headless CI job Architect Arch D3
    6 Extraction pipeline with batches and human review routing Architect Arch D4, D5
    7 Multi-agent research system with provenance Architect Arch D1, D2, D5
    8 RAG with an eval harness, and an architecture decision pack Professional Pro D3 to D6

    API usage is billed separately from a Claude Pro plan. Budget about $20 to $50 in Console credits for all eight builds. Use the smallest model that does the job, and use batches where a task allows it.

    0.6 Rules for this site

    • No exam content, ever. This repo is public, and every candidate signs a non-disclosure agreement (R1, section 13). After an exam, I do not add, reword or hint at anything I saw. The Drills page is written from the public blueprints and my builds only.
    • No employer or client names.
    • Do not copy the sample questions from the exam guides. Link to the guides instead.
    • Every fact carries a source and a check date. Re-read the exam guide in the week before each exam, in case the version has changed.
    • When the docs and the exam guide disagree, the exam guide wins for exam purposes. The guides predate some current models and features.

    Exam day

    Before every exam

    • [ ] Exam guide re-read in the week before. Version number checked against the one this site used.
    • [ ] Online proctoring: system test run on the exam computer, on the exam network, at least 2 days before (R8).
    • [ ] Government ID ready. Name matches the Pearson profile.
    • [ ] Desk clear. No second monitor, no phone in reach, no notes.
    • [ ] Light review only on the day before. Recall drills from Drills, no new topics.

    In the exam

    Each item states how many answers to select. Read that number first. Scenario items reward design judgment: prefer a programmatic control over a stronger instruction, least privilege over logging, and the managed feature over a hand-built one, unless the scenario rules it out.

    Plan for about 135 minutes in the seat, including check-in. Flag and move on after 2 minutes on one item.

    After every exam

    • [ ] Record the scaled score and the percent correct per domain in cert-lab/results.md. That file is private.
    • [ ] Pass: claim the Credly badge. Fail: book the earliest retake and start 30 minutes a day on the two weakest domains.
    • [ ] Update the next block's day plan with the domain results, before starting it.
