    Resources

    Every link the daily plan uses, numbered so a day can say "R24"

    All links were opened on 14 October 2026. Official Anthropic sources come first.

    Anthropic's own courses are the baseline for this site. Each day's Learn step names one. Docs pages are reference during builds. Third-party material appears only where Anthropic has nothing on the topic.

    Two Anthropic training sites appear below. Both run on the Skilljar platform and need a free Skilljar account, which is separate from a Claude account.

    Site What it is Who can use it
    Anthropic Partner Academy Training for staff at Claude Partner Network firms. It holds the exam prep courses (R12 to R16), the certification registration links, exam guides and partner webinars Partner staff only. Sign in with a company email on the partner's domain (R7)
    Claude Academy Anthropic's public course and tutorial catalog: API, Claude Platform, Claude Code, MCP, Skills, Subagents and AI Fluency (R17 to R22, R91, R92, R98, R99, R101, R102, R108 to R113). Its older address, anthropic.skilljar.com, still works Anyone. Free

    The Claude Partner Network is Anthropic's program for consulting and services firms. Partner tier sets the exam discount (R6).

    Source grades: [P] primary (Anthropic, Pearson). [S] credible secondary. Secondary sources are used only where no primary page states the fact.
