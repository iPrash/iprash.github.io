Five things that are time-critical

Two of these expire Week 0 — before any analysis starts
  • 01 Fix the renewal date and contract vehicle Ask for the order form and master agreement. Note whether ChatGPT Enterprise and the API platform sit on one paper or two. Blocking
  • 02 Request OpenAI admin access Admin API key, Global Admin Console analytics, billing invoices. Approvals take days — raise on day 1, not week 2. Blocking
  • 03 Export the Copilot trial data before it is unreachable Billing usage reports and metrics history for a cancelled subscription do not stay available. Missing trial data is the most likely cause of a weak Workstream 2. Expiring
  • 04 Check the model retirement calendar GPT-5.4 and GPT-5.4 mini retire in Codex for ChatGPT-signed-in users on 31 Aug 2026, replaced by GPT-5.6 Terra and Luna. Pinned workspace defaults and automations break. —
  • 05 Ask about weekly role-based limits Remaining ChatGPT Enterprise workspaces migrate to monthly usage limits during August 2026. A migration mid-baseline distorts period comparisons. Record the date it happens. Data quality

Operating principles

01
Evidence over assertionEvery number carries a source, a date and a confidence grade.
02
Decisions, not documentsEach deliverable ends with a recommended decision, an owner and a date.
03
Show the workingPublish the model and the assumptions. Let the client change one and watch the answer move.
04
No surprises upwardBad news travels immediately, with an option attached.
05
Leave the capability behindEvery model, dashboard and register handed over with a run-book.
06
Separate reversible from irreversibleMove fast on reversible choices. Slow down for contract terms, data flows and exit costs.
Regulatory clock · playbook §7.1

What already applies

2 Aug 2026 · live now

Article 50 transparency

Disclose AI interaction and AI-generated or manipulated content. Not deferred. For a fashion brand generating campaign imagery, this is live exposure this quarter.

2 Dec 2026 · imminent

Marking rules and new prohibitions

Article 50(2) marking extends to systems already on the market, and the new prohibitions take effect.

2 Dec 2027 / 2 Aug 2028

High-risk obligations

Deferred by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744. Recruitment tooling is the likely Annex III exposure — the clock is running.
