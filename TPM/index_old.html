Every resource below is free and mapped to a requirement from the job description. Work top to bottom, open each link, and tick it off — the bar up top tracks how far along you are.

Track 01

Technical depth

System design, cloud, scalability. Lead the conversation and reason about trade-offs.

Track 02

Program sense

Roadmaps, dependencies, risk, delivery. Where senior offers are won or lost.

Track 03

Leadership

Influence without authority, align stakeholders, communicate up the chain.

Before you start: your level is judged by the scope of the programs you describe — team count, dependencies, blast radius, business impact. As you read, collect 4–6 detailed stories you can tell with real numbers.
